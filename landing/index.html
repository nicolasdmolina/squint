Built for the person who has to make the call

  • Evidence you can point to

    Flagged passages are marked on your text. In the Mac app, tap any word to see what the model expected there and where the real word ranked.

  • Honest about doubt

    Under 150 words you get a low confidence label. Under 50 you get no verdict at all.

  • Fair to English learners

    Plain or formulaic writing isn’t treated as cheating. Learner essays are measured as their own group.

  • Private by default

    Runs on one Mac mini with no cloud AI. Nothing you paste is saved, and results are gone after 10 minutes.

How it works

  1. Paste or upload

    Type, paste, or drop in a .docx, .pdf, or .txt file. Up to 500 words and 3 checks an hour here; 2,500 words and no limit in the Mac app.

  2. A trained classifier reads it

    ModernBERT, fine tuned on human, ChatGPT, and Claude writing, gives the verdict and checks the text in overlapping 150 word passages.

  3. Read the evidence

    Likely human, Uncertain, or Likely AI, the AI written share, and the passages behind it. The Mac app adds Binoculars: two Falcon-7B models rate every word.

What it still gets wrong

  • College essays by real students, from a source it never trained on, are wrongly flagged about 4 to 6% of the time.
  • Some Claude writing gets through, mostly under 150 words: about 90% caught there, 95% at 150 words and up.
  • Older models are mostly missed. 2022 era ChatGPT answers got through almost every time.
  • Edited or paraphrased AI text is untested, and so is Gemini.

Questions

Is a “Likely AI” result proof?

No. It means the text reads like current ChatGPT or Claude writing. Treat it as a reason to look closer and talk to the writer, never as a verdict on its own.

How much text do I need?

At least 20 words to check, 50 for a verdict, and 150 for the AI written share. Results are most reliable from 150 words up. This site takes up to 500 words a check.

Do you store what I paste?

No. Text is checked in memory on one Mac mini and never written to disk. Results are dropped after 10 minutes, and no third party AI service sees your text. The Mac app never sends it anywhere.

Will it flag writers who are still learning English?

It wrongly flagged 1 of 512 learner essays in testing, and 0 of 600 on a fresh set. Simple or formulaic writing is not treated as a sign of AI on its own. College essays are the weak spot: about 4 to 6% are wrongly flagged.

Which AI models can it detect?

Current ChatGPT and Claude. Older models are mostly missed, and Gemini hasn’t been tested yet. The known gaps are listed in the app next to every verdict.

Why don’t I see every word marked?

The word marks come from two 7 billion parameter models, too heavy to run for everyone on one Mac mini. This site runs the classifier and marks whole passages. The Mac app runs both on your own computer.

Why is there a limit of 3 checks an hour?

Every check runs on one Mac mini at home. The limit keeps it free for everyone. The Mac app has no limit.
