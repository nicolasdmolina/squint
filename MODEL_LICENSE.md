## Base models

- **ModernBERT-base** (Answer.AI and LightOn, Apache-2.0): the classifier is fine-tuned from it.
- **Falcon-7B and Falcon-7B-Instruct** (Technology Innovation Institute, Apache-2.0), 4-bit GGUF
  quantizations by mradermacher: the Binoculars model pair, downloaded from Hugging Face.
- **Binoculars** method: Hans et al., "Spotting LLMs With Binoculars: Zero-Shot Detection of
  Machine-Generated Text", ICML 2024.
